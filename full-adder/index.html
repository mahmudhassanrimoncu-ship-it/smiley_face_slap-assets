The two rules

  • Sum is on when an odd number of inputs are on.
    S = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin
  • Carry is on when two or more inputs are on.
    Cout = A·B + Cin·(A ⊕ B)

Pinout (all three chips)

  • gate 1: in 1, 2 → out 3
  • gate 2: in 4, 5 → out 6
  • pin 14 = VCC (+5 V) · pin 7 = GND
  • notch left → pin 1 bottom-left, count counter-clockwise

Parts

  • 74LS86 or 74HC86 (quad XOR)
  • 74LS08 or 74HC08 (quad AND)
  • 74LS32 or 74HC32 (quad OR)
  • 20 jumper wires, trainer kit with +5 V, switches, LEDs
  • HC chips: tie unused inputs 9, 10, 12, 13 to GND
