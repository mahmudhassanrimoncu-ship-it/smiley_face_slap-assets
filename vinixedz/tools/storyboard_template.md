## How to read each scene

- **Duration:** measured from the rendered file. It is independent of your voice-over speed, because each sentence card holds for a fixed reading time, not a spoken time.
- **Narration and subtitle:** the exact sentences shown at the bottom, in order. The same text is in `04-narration.md`.
- **Motion graphics:** written so you can rebuild or extend the scene in After Effects. Easing notation:
  - *expo-out* ≈ AE Easy Ease Out with 85–95% influence on the outgoing handle
  - *cubic in-out* ≈ Easy Ease with 60–70% influence on both handles
  - *quint in-out* ≈ 80%+ on both handles (whip-pan feel)
- **Global visual system:**
  - Near-black navy backdrop (#06080D) with a slow-drifting perspective floor grid, depth-parallax dust and a vignette.
  - The backdrop is driven by **global time**, so it never jumps at a cut. Every scene boundary is a seamless continuation.
  - Accent colours: cyan #3FE3FF, magenta #FF3D7F, amber #FFB648, green #4CF0A0, violet #8F7DFF.
  - Type: Inter 500–900, JetBrains Mono for data and labels.
- **Evidence pills:** green VERIFIED, cyan OBSERVED, amber HYPOTHESIS, magenta SPECULATION, grey NOTE. They sit above the subtitle bar, with bibliography numbers.
- **Footage slots:** hatched frames labelled `FOOTAGE SLOT xx` mark where real footage goes. See `07-footage-and-archival.md`.
