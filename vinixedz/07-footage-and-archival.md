# Footage and archival plan

The rendered video contains **footage slots**: hatched frames labelled `FOOTAGE SLOT xx`. Replace each one with real material in your editor, using the slot as a position and size guide. Slot positions and first timecodes are listed in the table at the end, which is generated from the render log.

## Usage principles

- **Commentary and analysis.** Use short excerpts (typically 2–6 s) that are directly discussed on screen. Do not use full edits, and do not let the music from creators' edits play under your narration for long stretches.
- **Credit every clip** on screen with the channel name and handle, bottom-left, in JetBrains Mono 18 px at 70% white.
- **Ask first where you can.** Message each creator before publishing: it is courteous, and it can turn into interviews or behind-the-scenes material.
- **No personal details.** Show channel names and handles only. Some creators in this space appear to be young, so never show faces they have not made public, and never show ages or locations.
- **Archival material:** prefer the Wayback Machine, official press images (Adobe, Video Copilot, Topaz press kits) and your own screen recordings of software.
- **Fair use / fair dealing** depends on the jurisdiction and isn't automatic. When in doubt, use fewer and shorter excerpts and more of your own demonstrations.
